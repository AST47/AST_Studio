AST STUDIO LOGO PLACEHOLDER
============================

To use your custom logo design:
1. Save your custom graphic as: public/logo.png
2. Transparent PNG background recommended (e.g. 240x80px or 400x120px)
3. AST Studio will automatically detect and display it in the header!

Studio Color Palette for designing:
- Indigo: #6366f1
- Violet: #8b5cf6
- Purple: #a855f7
- Neon Pink: #ec4899
- Background Dark: #0a0a0a
- Linear Gradient: linear-gradient(135deg, #6366f1 0%, #8b5cf6 35%, #a855f7 70%, #ec4899 100%)
